# Scarline home surface

Mode: experience, plain-language. Audience: general readers aged 13 to 15, phone or laptop. Job: understand in a minute that a simple method failed here, and why.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a survey sheet. The page reads like an official Australian survey plate: honest, measured, nothing oversold.

OWN-WORLD: pale grey-blue paper (#dfe5ea), ink-black type and hairline rules, numbered plates with scale bar and north arrow, grid-reference captions. One signal colour, rust (#c2501c graphics, #9c3d12 text), only for burnt change. Georgia headings, system-ui body, 18px, 1.6 line height, 60-72 character measure. No gradients, glass, cards, emoji or shadows.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the title, the honesty line ("Our simple method didn't work here...") and the "Negative result" label, with the before plate arriving below.

VISITOR PATH: title and honesty line, plates 1-3, sections 1 to 6 in reading order, sources, specialists link.

SIGNATURE: the before/after plates. One aria-pressed button flips July and October on phones (150 ms fade, none under reduced motion); side by side on wide screens. Scale bar and north arrow sit on every picture.

HONEST RISK: a paper-and-ink look can feel dry for teenagers. Plain words, short sentences and "What does this mean?" notes carry the warmth, not decoration.
